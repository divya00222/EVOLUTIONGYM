FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about joining Evolution Gym in Kathmandu.

Q. How do I sign up or confirm current membership rates?
You can sign up in person at our front desk in Kathmandu, or call us directly at 981-5813774. You can also claim a free 1-day pass online to visit us first.
Q. Are there special student rates available?
Yes! Evolution Gym offers special student discounts for college and high school students in Kathmandu. Bring your valid student ID card when visiting our front desk.
Q. Can I try the gym before committing to a plan?
Absolutely. We offer a 100% complimentary 1-day pass so you can test our dumbbells, machines, and floor atmosphere with zero obligation.
Q. What are your operating hours?
We are open Monday through Saturday from 5:30 AM to 10:00 PM, and Sunday from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
Q. Is trainer guidance included with my membership?
Yes, every membership includes general floor trainer support for posture corrections, spotting, and equipment safety. Dedicated 1-on-1 personal training can be added as a separate package.
Q. How do I contact the gym for membership pricing?
Contact the gym for current membership pricing at Phone: 981-5813774. Our front desk staff is happy to guide you through current seasonal offers!
